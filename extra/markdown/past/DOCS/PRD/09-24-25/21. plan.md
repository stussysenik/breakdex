Here's a step-by-step breakdown of what's happening and where the logic fails:

2.  **State is Correctly Updated:** The `AddMoveUnifiedState` correctly stores the trim times relative to the original video (`trimStartTime = 0.365`). The `currentPlayerViewModel` in the `unifiedPlayerManager` is successfully updated with the new, trimmed `AVPlayerItem`.

3.  **Transition to Naming View:** The app transitions to `NameMoveViewUnified`. The view correctly receives the `unifiedState` containing the updated player view model.

By trying to seek to `trimStartTime`, you are telling it to jump to a point that correctly corresponds to the original video's timeline but is incorrect for the new clip's timeline.

This incorrect seek is why you see the "Seeking to start..." message. The seek operation likely fails or times out because the player is trying to move to a time that makes sense for the old context but not the new one.
