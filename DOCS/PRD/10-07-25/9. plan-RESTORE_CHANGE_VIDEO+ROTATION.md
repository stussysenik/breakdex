### \#\# 🕵️ Root Cause Analysis: A Tale of Two Morphisms

Your analysis in `7. fix-CHANGE_VIDEO_FUNC.md` is spot on. We have two different morphisms (code paths) intended to achieve the same outcome (video loaded and ready for trimming), but one is simple and correct, while the other is complex, over-engineered, and broken.

    `PhotosPicker selection → didSelectVideo(item)`

    `Button Tap → performAtomicVideoReplacement(item) → prepareForNewVideoSelection() → [LOCK FAILED]`

      * **Log Evidence:** `🔒 TransitionLockManager: ❌ Lock acquisition failed - Transition lock already acquired by operation: 436E0CF5-BECC-44CE-B59F-16B8761EC12A`

**Categorical Interpretation:**
This is a **non-commutative diagram** of state transitions and lock management. The sequence `releaseLock ∘ transitionToTrimming` is not being correctly composed. The `transitionToTrimming` morphism completes, but the subsequent `releaseLock` morphism is never called, leaving the `TransitionLockManager` object in a permanently locked state. Any subsequent operation requiring the lock will fail.

The over-engineering in `FeatureRichTrimmerView`'s "Change Video" handler attempts to perform an action (`prepareForNewVideoSelection`) that is already the responsibility of the central state machine, violating the Single Responsibility Principle (SRP).
