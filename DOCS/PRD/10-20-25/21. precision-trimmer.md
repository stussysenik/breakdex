## \#\# Current System's Behavior (Floating Handles & Incomplete Fill)

The root cause is that the **blue highlight** (`Capsule()`) is sized and positioned based on the coordinates returned by `timeToCoordinate`, which now represent positions along the *available track* (`totalWidth - handleWidth`). However, the **handles** (`Circle()`) are positioned by taking that same coordinate and *adding back* the handle radius (`handleWidth / 2.0`). This creates a mismatch.

**Evidence & Step-by-Step Analysis:**

1.  **Coordinate System:** Your `timeToCoordinate` function now correctly maps time (`0` to `videoDuration`) to a pixel coordinate within the *available track width* (`0px` to `totalWidth - handleWidth`). Let's call this `coordinateOnTrack`.

2.  **Handle Positioning:** The handles are positioned by offsetting their **centers** to `coordinateOnTrack + (handleWidth / 2.0)`.

      * At `time = 0s`, `coordinateOnTrack` is `0px`. The handle center is offset to `0 + radius = radius`. This correctly places the handle's left edge at `0px`. 👍
      * At `time = videoDuration`, `coordinateOnTrack` is `availableWidth`. The handle center is offset to `availableWidth + radius = totalWidth - radius`. This correctly places the handle's right edge at `totalWidth`. 👍

3.  **Blue Highlight Sizing & Positioning:** The blue highlight is created using an `HStack` with three rectangles:

      * **Left Spacer (`Rectangle()`):** Width = `timeToCoordinate(startTime, ...)`. This is `coordinateOnTrack` for the start time.
      * **Blue Fill (`Capsule()`):** Width = `timeToCoordinate(endTime - startTime, ...)`. This calculation is flawed because `timeToCoordinate` is designed for absolute times, not durations, within the available track context. It effectively calculates `coordinateOnTrack(endTime) - coordinateOnTrack(startTime)`.
      * **Right Spacer (`Rectangle()`):** Implicitly fills the remaining space.

4.  **The Discrepancy:**

      * **Start:** When `startTime` is `0s`, the Left Spacer's width is `timeToCoordinate(0s) = 0px`. The blue highlight starts immediately at `x=0px`. However, the left handle's *center* is at `x=radius`, and its *left edge* is at `x=0px`. The blue highlight starts *inside* the handle's center, creating the gap/float.
      * **End:** The blue highlight's width is calculated as `coordinateOnTrack(endTime) - coordinateOnTrack(startTime)`. When `endTime` is `videoDuration`, this width is `availableWidth - coordinateOnTrack(startTime)`. The total width occupied by the Left Spacer and the Blue Fill is `coordinateOnTrack(startTime) + (availableWidth - coordinateOnTrack(startTime)) = availableWidth`. This means the blue highlight ends at `x=availableWidth`. However, the right handle's *center* is at `x = availableWidth + radius`, and its *right edge* is at `x = totalWidth`. The blue highlight ends *inside* the right handle's center.
