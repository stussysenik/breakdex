⏺ ProgressTypes (VideoLoadingState.swift) are the traffic lights that tell users exactly what's happening during video
  loading - like "downloading from iCloud" at 60% or "validating file" at 90%.

  UnifiedState (AddMoveUnifiedState) is the master control center that stores everything about the current video operation
  - the selected video, trim start/end times, and loading progress - keeping all components synchronized across the app.
