### Targeting iPhone 13 and Later

For iOS app development targeting iPhone 13 and newer models, focus on logical screen dimensions in points (pt), which account for Retina scaling and ensure adaptive layouts via Auto Layout and size classes. These dimensions vary slightly by model due to bezel changes and display advancements, but common portrait orientations range from 360x780 pt (smaller models like the iPhone 13 mini) to 440x956 pt (larger Pro Max variants), with most falling into 390-430 pt widths for standard 6.1-6.7 inch screens [1][2][3][4][5].
