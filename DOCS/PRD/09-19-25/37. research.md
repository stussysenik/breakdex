### The True Final Issue: A Subtle Asynchronicity Bug

So, what is the real problem? The logs reveal a subtle but critical bug in `AddMoveViewModel.swift` related to asynchronous operations and state management.

Here's the sequence of events that's causing the failure:

1.  **Video Loaded**: `AddMoveViewModel.loadVideo` successfully loads the full video asset and creates a player item from it. It then transitions to the `.previewing` state. **At this point, you have a player with the *full, untrimmed* video.**
2.  **User Trims**: The user trims the video and taps "Done." `AddMoveViewModel` transitions to the `.naming` state, passing along the *original full asset* and the new trim times.
3.  **Preview Generation**: The `NameMoveView.task` modifier kicks in. It calls `VideoTransformBuilder.createPlayerItem` with the *original full asset* and the trim times.
4.  **The Race Condition**: `VideoTransformBuilder` correctly builds a new, trimmed `AVMutableComposition`. However, there's a possibility that the original `AVURLAsset` passed into it is not fully "ready" or has resources tied up by the previous player in the `.previewing` state.
