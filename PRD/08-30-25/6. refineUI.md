#### ✅ **2. Magnifying Loupe & Precision Trimming**

*   **Implement a Magnifying Loupe:** Instead of a static preview, a circular loupe appears directly above the timeline handle *only* when you are actively dragging it.
*   **Isolate Drag States (No Jitter):** Do not use a single, global `isDragging` state. Create two separate `@State` booleans to handle each drag gesture independently.
    ```swift
    @State private var isDraggingStartHandle = false
    @State private var isDraggingEndHandle = false
    ```
*   **Create Two Independent Loupes:** Your view will contain two `MagnifyingLoupeView` instances.
    *   One loupe's visibility and position are tied *only* to the start handle and `isDraggingStartHandle`.
    *   The second loupe's visibility and position are tied *only* to the end handle and `isDraggingEndHandle`.
*   **Frame-Accurate Preview:** The content inside the loupe must be a live, frame-accurate preview that updates instantly as the handle is dragged. The main video player should also seek in real-time.

#### ✅ **3. Performance & Polish**

*   **Eliminate the Final Re-render:** Do not call `player.seek()` in the `.onEnded` block of your drag gesture. The player is already at the correct position from the continuous drag updates, and this extra call is what causes the final jitter.
*   **Asynchronous Thumbnails:** Generate the filmstrip of thumbnails on a background thread when the view first loads. This prevents the UI from stuttering upon entry.
*   **Smart Animations:** Apply a gentle spring animation *only* to the appearance and disappearance of each loupe. Do not animate the entire view, which can degrade performance.
    ```swift
    // Example for one loupe
    MagnifyingLoupeView()
        .opacity(isDraggingStartHandle ? 1 : 0)
        .animation(.spring(duration: 0.2), value: isDraggingStartHandle)
    ```
*   **Add Haptic Feedback:** Provide a light haptic tap (`.light`) the moment a loupe appears and disappears. This makes the interaction feel intentional, polished, and physically responsive.
