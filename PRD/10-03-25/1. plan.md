# Main functions for navigating back

The main functions responsible for video player reinitialization when navigating from namemoveview to trimmerview are:

Primary Functions:
- `FlowStateManager.rollbackToTrimming() (FlowStateManager.swift:175-356)`
- `FlowStateManager.attemptDirectTrimmingStateRestoration() (FlowStateManager.swift:895-1066)`

Supporting Functions:
- `FlowStateManager.synchronizeTrimmingStateToUnifiedState() (FlowStateManager.swift:542-590)`
- `FlowStateManager.proceedToNextState() (FlowStateManager.swift:50-144)`

The reinitialization process works through state preservation mechanisms that capture the trimmer state before navigation and restore it when returning, ensuring video player continuity during rapid view transitions.

# Video Player recreation

After the state management functions are triggered, the actual video player recreation is handled by these key functions:

Primary Player Creation:
- `UnifiedPlayerManager.createOrUpdatePlayer() (UnifiedPlayerManager.swift:42-158)` - Creates new AVPlayer instances and UnifiedVideoPlayerViewModel wrappers

Asset Processing:
- `VideoTransformBuilder.createPlayerItem() (VideoTransformBuilder.swift:335-391)` - Creates transformed AVPlayerItem instances with rotation/trimming

Trimmer Setup:
- `TrimmerViewModel.setupAsync() (TrimmerViewModel.swift:573-760)` - Initializes video duration, tracks, and trim ranges

Player Wrapper Initialization:
- `UnifiedVideoPlayerViewModel.init() (UnifiedVideoPlayerViewModel.swift:99-132)` - Wraps AVPlayer in observable view models with health monitoring

Fallback Creation:
- `FlowStateManager.createTrimmerViewModelForFallback() (FlowStateManager.swift:361-435)` - Creates new TrimmerViewModel instances during rollback scenarios. The actual video player objects (AVPlayer and AVPlayerItem) are created in UnifiedPlayerManager and VideoTransformBuilder, then wrapped and configured through the other functions.
