## **Spec-Kit: WYSIWYG Video Preview in Naming View**

This spec outlines the goal, verifiable criteria, and implementation plan for fixing a critical bug in the "Add Move" flow. Your task is to ensure the video preview on the naming screen is a true WYSIWYG representation of the user's edits.
