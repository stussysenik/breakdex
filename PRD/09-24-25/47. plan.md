### 🎯 Direct Answer: The Root Cause

The blank video screen occurs because the `AVPlayerViewRepresentable`, which hosts the video player layer, is not aware that the `AVPlayer`'s content has changed.

In your `proceedToNextState` function, you correctly replace the player's *content* (the `AVPlayerItem`) with a new trimmed and rotated `AVComposition`. However, the `AVPlayer` *instance itself* remains the same. SwiftUI's view diffing mechanism sees that the `AVPlayer` object passed to the view is unchanged (`viewModel.avPlayer === oldViewModel.avPlayer`) and therefore doesn't trigger the necessary `updateUIView` call to reconfigure the view for the new video content.

This creates a stale render: the `NameMoveView` is constructed with a player that, from SwiftUI's perspective, hasn't changed, leaving the underlying `AVPlayerLayer` in an un-rendered or invalid state for the new composition.

### ⚙️ Solution: Forcing State Synchronization

To fix this, we need to make the view explicitly dependent on the `AVPlayerItem`, not just the `AVPlayer`. By passing the `playerItem` as a parameter to the `AVPlayerViewRepresentable`, we create a direct dependency that SwiftUI can track. When the item is replaced, SwiftUI will detect the change and correctly call `updateUIView`, allowing us to re-synchronize the player layer.
