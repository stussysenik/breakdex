### The Definitive Fix

The solution is to replace the flawed ease-in calculation with a standard, robust animation technique called linear interpolation (lerp). This will move the progress bar by a small fraction of the *remaining distance* each frame, which is mathematically sound and also produces a pleasing ease-out effect.
