-> Needless Complexity: The app had two competing state management systems (AddMoveState enum and AddMoveAppState class), which created confusion and potential synchronization errors.

-> How the New Architecture Solves This
The refactor replaced that flawed model with a stable, State-First Architecture.

Before: Multiple, temporary state objects and players were created and destroyed constantly.

After: A single, persistent AddMoveUnifiedState object and a single, persistent UnifiedPlayerManager now live through the entire "Add Move" flow.

This new, stable foundation ensures that the video player and its state are preserved across all views. It eliminates the performance issues and guarantees that the final, transformed video you see in the trimmer is the exact same one you see in the naming view, making the entire process reliable and predictable.
