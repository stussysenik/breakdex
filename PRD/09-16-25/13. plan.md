### **Engineering Spec: Resolve `AVPlayerItem` Ownership Crash by Unifying the Video Loading Pipeline**

### **1. Objective**

To fundamentally resolve the `NSInvalidArgumentException` crash by refactoring the Add Move flow. This will be achieved by:

1.  **Eliminating the redundant loading process** in `PreTrimContainerView`.
2.  **Making `AddMoveViewModel` the single source of truth** for loading the video and creating the player.
3.  **Modifying the `VideoLoadingService` API** to return a shareable `AVAsset` instead of a player-specific `AVPlayerItem`.

The end goal is a stable, efficient, single-pass loading pipeline that correctly displays the video in `PreTrimView`.

### **2. Background**

The current implementation crashes with the error: `'An AVPlayerItem cannot be associated with more than one instance of AVPlayer'`. Our diagnostic logs confirm the cause: the `VideoLoadingService` creates its own `AVPlayer` to manage an `AVPlayerItem`. The `UnifiedVideoPlayerViewModel` then receives this item and attempts to assign it to its own, separate `AVPlayer` instance, which is a violation of AVFoundation rules.

This is a symptom of a larger architectural flaw: the video is being loaded twice. First by `AddMoveViewModel` to get the asset, and then again by `PreTrimContainerView` to populate the player. This spec consolidates this into a single, correct loading sequence.
